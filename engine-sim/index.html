The failure modes

Break it like you mean it.

Pool fuel in a cold combustor and torch it. Slam a hydromechanical J85 and ride the compressor into surge. Vapor-lock gasoline at 40 °C, gel diesel at −15 °C, bounce off the rev limiter. Each fault is simulated, logged, and recoverable.

  • wet starts & EGT spikes
  • compressor surge & lean blowout
  • vapor lock · cold gel · knock

How it works

From cold metal to redline in three steps.

01

Pick an engine

Forty-six presets across four engine families: gasoline, diesel, LPG pistons, two supercharged radial aero engines, a one-to-four-rotor Wankel lineup, and three turbojets. Or start from one and make it yours.

02

Start it properly

Glow plugs on a cold diesel. Choke on a carbureted four. Fuel cock and igniters on the J85. Real engines have start procedures, and so do these.

03

Tune it while it runs

Bore, stroke, rotor geometry, compression, mass flow, nozzle area: every spec is live-editable. The sound, the dyno, and the failure modes respond as you drag.
