BEATS BY GRAVES

An entire music studio that fits in 184 KB of HTML.

A subtractive synth with 57 presets, a 7-channel step sequencer, a piano roll with real-song chord, melody and riff generators, a per-channel effects rack, a playlist arranger, and WAV export — running on the Web Audio API with zero dependencies. No install, no sign-up, no build.

Every sound you hear is generated in real time — there isn't a single sample in the file. A from-scratch subtractive synthesizer (dual oscillators, unison, sub, noise, FM, multi-mode filter, LFO, drive and built-in chorus/delay/reverb) drives a channel-rack step sequencer, a piano roll with musically-aware chord, melody and riff dice, and a playlist arranger with mixdown to WAV.

It's proof of how much is possible when you can count to potato.
